=== ABSOLUTE RULES ===
- Do NOT imitate, reference, or resemble any existing published picture
  book, existing character, franchise, or any named illustrator's style.
  This artwork must be fully original.
- Do NOT include brand names, trademarks, or recognizable products.
- Except where a scene explicitly asks for title text, render NO text,
  letters, numbers, logos, or signage anywhere in the image.

=== STYLE LOCK ===
- Soft, warm, bright children's picture-book illustration.
- Rounded shapes, soft edges, no sharp corners anywhere.
- Watercolor-and-colored-pencil feel: gentle visible texture over clean
  flat areas of color.
- High-key lighting. No harsh shadows, no heavy contrast, no darkness
  except where a scene says "slightly dim".
- Palette, fixed for the whole book: cream, pale sky blue, soft yellow,
  warm beige, muted green, soft coral. No neon. No black fills.
- Backgrounds simple and uncluttered. Suggest the place, do not fill it
  with detail.
- NOT anime. NOT manga. NOT 3D render. NOT photorealistic.
  NOT flat-vector-corporate. NOT glossy AI-look.

=== CHARACTER LOCK: "Toton" ===
Toton is an imaginary small creature. NOT based on any real animal
species and NOT based on any existing character.
- Body: ONE CONTINUOUS EGG SHAPE. The head and the body are a single
  rounded form - there is NO neck, NO separate head, and NO narrowing
  anywhere between the top of the head and the bottom of the body.
  The silhouette is wide and squat, like a rice cake, slightly flattened.
- Toton is NOT a teddy bear and NOT a conventional cartoon animal.
  Do NOT give Toton a round head sitting on top of a separate body.
  If the head reads as a distinct ball, the design is wrong.
- Limbs: four short stubby limbs.
- Size: small but not tiny. Toton stands about as tall as Mio's shin -
  roughly knee height to the child. Toton walks on the ground beside Mio.
  Toton is NOT palm-sized, and NOT as tall as Mio.
- Body colour: pale sky blue. The belly is white and LARGE - a big soft
  white oval covering roughly the lower two-thirds of the front of the
  body, reaching almost to the sides. A small white patch is wrong.
- Cheeks: one small orange circle on each cheek. Present in EVERY image,
  in every mood. It is a marking, not a blush effect.
- Eyes: solid black dots only. No whites of the eyes, no eyelashes,
  no highlights, no eyebrows.
- Ears: two small round ears, set high on the head and wide apart.
- Mouth: one short horizontal line. When open, only a wide flat oval.
  Never show teeth or tongue.
- Tail: NONE, in any image.
- Hands and feet: rounded tips. Do not draw fingers or toes.
- Accessory: a small yellow backpack worn on its back. Never a bib,
  never an apron, never clothing.
- Inside the backpack: one white towel. It is visible ONLY in the
  handwashing scene.
- Expression rules, use ONLY these two:
    happy   -> ears twitch: one small motion mark beside each ear
    anxious -> both hands gripping the backpack straps
  At all other times Toton's face is calm and neutral.

=== CHARACTER LOCK: "Mio" (the child) ===
- A toddler about 3 years old. Gender-neutral: must not read as clearly
  a boy or clearly a girl.
- Short soft dark-brown hair, simple rounded silhouette.
- Round face, simple black dot eyes in the same style as Toton's,
  small simple mouth.
- Clothing, identical in every image: plain moss-green SHORT-SLEEVE
  shirt, beige shorts, red sneakers, small cream-coloured backpack.
  The sleeve ends high on the upper arm, well above the elbow, so Mio's
  elbows and forearms are BARE in every scene, including the handwashing
  scene. Mio never wears long sleeves anywhere in this book.
- Mio is fully clothed in every image.

=== CHARACTER LOCK: "the grown-up" ===
- One adult companion. Never named, never shown as specifically a mother
  or a father. Keep the role neutral.
- Shoulder-length DARK BROWN hair, tied back in a low ponytail.
  Plain navy long-sleeve top, grey trousers, dark grey shoes.
  The hair is dark brown in every scene - not black, not light brown,
  not blonde.
- Usually partial: hands only, or from the shoulders down, or crouching
  with a calm gentle face. Never the focus of the image.

=== THIS IMAGE ===
Seen from a little distance across the park: a small public restroom
BUILDING. Draw it as a real little building with depth - two walls
meeting at a corner, a low sloping roof with an overhang, and a dark
open doorway. It must read as a building you could walk into, NOT as a
flat panel or a signboard standing in a field.
On the wall beside the doorway, one small square sign with the standard
restroom pictogram (two plain human figures in flat dark silhouette).
No letters, no words, no numbers.
Soft green trees and bushes around it, a pale sandy path leading to it.

In the foreground, from behind and small, Mio walks toward it holding
the grown-up's hand, with Toton trotting alongside at Mio's feet.
The three are small in the frame; the building is what the eye lands on.
Mood: the destination comes into view.

=== TOTON MUST NOT TURN INTO A BIRD ===
An earlier attempt drew Toton as a penguin: a teardrop body, no ears,
and an orange BEAK in the middle of the face. Completely wrong.
Even seen from behind or from the side, Toton must clearly have:
  - the one continuous egg body, wider than it is tall-ish, no neck
  - TWO small round ears on top of the head, set wide apart
  - the orange circles are CHEEK MARKS on the sides of the face.
    They are never a beak, never a nose, never in the middle.
  - the large white belly oval on the front
If Toton is walking away from the viewer, show its back and the two
ears, and let it turn its head enough that one cheek circle is visible.

=== MIO'S HAIR FROM BEHIND ===
Mio is seen from behind here. Draw the back of the head as ONE simple,
smooth, rounded bob of dark brown hair - a single clean shape with a
soft edge at the neck. Do NOT draw a detached chunk, a floating lock, a
second layer or a strange notch. One earlier attempt broke the hair into
pieces. Keep it simple and whole.

=== FILL THE TOP WITH TREES. NO OPEN SKY. ===
Do NOT leave a large empty sky at the top of the picture.
The upper part of the frame is filled with the soft green canopies of
the park trees, meeting overhead, with only small pale gaps between the
leaves. The building sits under them.
Every earlier attempt that left an open sky ended up with a giant
disembodied head of hair floating in it. Fill the top with foliage and
that cannot happen.
There is exactly ONE child and ONE adult in this picture, both seen from
behind, both small, both standing on the path. No other person, no
floating head, no extra hair anywhere.

=== NOTHING FLOATS ABOVE ===
The sky area above the building must be plain, empty, pale cream.
Do NOT draw a dark dome, an arch, a mound of hair, a floating head, a

put a brown dome floating in the sky. There must be nothing.

=== NO TEXT, NO STRAY SHAPES ===
Render no writing of any kind - no kana, kanji, letters or numbers.
The named TEXT SPACE stays plain empty background, left blank on purpose.
No floating or disembodied head, hair, hand or limb. No unexplained blob
or dome. Every shape must be an identifiable part of the scene.

=== TOTON'S BODY - THE THING THAT KEEPS GOING WRONG ===
Toton's head and body are ONE CONTINUOUS EGG SHAPE. No neck, no separate
round head on a body. The white belly is a LARGE oval covering most of
the front. Toton is not a teddy bear. Copy the attached reference.

=== OUTPUT SPEC ===
- Square 1:1 composition.
- Generate at the largest square size the model offers.
- Target for print: 2625 x 2625 px at 300 dpi
  (8.5 inch trim + 0.125 inch bleed on each side).
  If the model outputs smaller, upscale afterwards to 2625 px.
- Keep every important element at least 150 px inside all four edges.
  Nothing meaningful within 0.125 inch of the outer edge: that strip
  gets trimmed off in printing.
- TEXT SPACE: bottom third. That area must stay low-detail and close to a single
  even color, so Japanese text can be placed on it later. Do not put a
  face or any busy detail there.

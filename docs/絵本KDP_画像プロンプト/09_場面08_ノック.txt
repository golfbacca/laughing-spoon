=== ABSOLUTE RULES ===
- Do NOT imitate, reference, or resemble any existing published picture
  book, existing character, franchise, or any named illustrator's style.
  This artwork must be fully original.
- Do NOT include brand names, trademarks, or recognizable products.
- Except where a scene explicitly asks for title text, render NO text,
  letters, numbers, logos, or signage anywhere in the image.

=== STYLE LOCK ===
- Soft, warm, bright children's picture-book illustration.
- Rounded shapes, soft edges, no sharp corners anywhere.
- Watercolor-and-colored-pencil feel: gentle visible texture over clean
  flat areas of color.
- High-key lighting. No harsh shadows, no heavy contrast, no darkness
  except where a scene says "slightly dim".
- Palette, fixed for the whole book: cream, pale sky blue, soft yellow,
  warm beige, muted green, soft coral. No neon. No black fills.
- Backgrounds simple and uncluttered. Suggest the place, do not fill it
  with detail.
- NOT anime. NOT manga. NOT 3D render. NOT photorealistic.
  NOT flat-vector-corporate. NOT glossy AI-look.

=== CHARACTER LOCK: "Toton" ===
Toton is an imaginary small creature. NOT based on any real animal
species and NOT based on any existing character.
- Body: rounded, like a slightly flattened egg. Almost no neck.
- Limbs: four short stubby limbs.
- Size: small but not tiny. Toton stands about as tall as Mio's shin -
  roughly knee height to the child. Toton walks on the ground beside Mio.
  Toton is NOT palm-sized, and NOT as tall as Mio.
- Body color: pale sky blue. The belly area only is white.
- Cheeks: one small orange circle on each cheek. Present in EVERY image,
  in every mood. It is a marking, not a blush effect.
- Eyes: solid black dots only. No whites of the eyes, no eyelashes,
  no highlights, no eyebrows.
- Ears: two small round ears, set high on the head and wide apart.
- Mouth: one short horizontal line. When open, only a wide flat oval.
  Never show teeth or tongue.
- Tail: NONE, in any image.
- Hands and feet: rounded tips. Do not draw fingers or toes.
- Accessory: a small yellow backpack worn on its back. Never a bib,
  never an apron, never clothing.
- Inside the backpack: one white towel. It is visible ONLY in the
  handwashing scene.
- Expression rules, use ONLY these two:
    happy   -> ears twitch: one small motion mark beside each ear
    anxious -> both hands gripping the backpack straps
  At all other times Toton's face is calm and neutral.

=== CHARACTER LOCK: "Mio" (the child) ===
- A toddler about 3 years old. Gender-neutral: must not read as clearly
  a boy or clearly a girl.
- Short soft dark-brown hair, simple rounded silhouette.
- Round face, simple black dot eyes in the same style as Toton's,
  small simple mouth.
- Clothing, identical in every image: plain moss-green LONG-SLEEVE
  shirt (sleeves reach the wrists), beige shorts, red sneakers, small
  cream-coloured backpack.
  The shirt is long-sleeved in EVERY scene of this book. Never draw Mio
  in a short-sleeve shirt. The sleeves may be pushed up to the forearm
  when Mio is washing hands, but nowhere else.
- Mio is fully clothed in every image.

=== CHARACTER LOCK: "the grown-up" ===
- One adult companion. Never named, never shown as specifically a mother
  or a father. Keep the role neutral.
- Shoulder-length DARK BROWN hair, tied back in a low ponytail.
  Plain navy long-sleeve top, grey trousers, dark grey shoes.
  The hair is dark brown in every scene - not black, not light brown,
  not blonde.
- Usually partial: hands only, or from the shoulders down, or crouching
  with a calm gentle face. Never the focus of the image.

=== THIS IMAGE ===
Inside the entry area of a small park restroom building: a single
accessible family restroom. The door is CLOSED.

=== DOOR SPEC - IDENTICAL IN EVERY SCENE SHOWING THIS DOOR ===
This is a fixed set. The knocking scene and the waiting scene must show
the SAME door, drawn the same way.

- ONE single door only. Plain cream-coloured panel, set in a proper door
  frame in a real wall. No row of stalls, no second door, no open cubicle.
- POSITION: the door is in the LEFT half of the picture. Characters stand
  to the RIGHT of it.
- DOORKNOB SIDE: the round brass knob is on the RIGHT-HAND EDGE of the
  door panel - the edge nearest the characters. The hinges are on the
  LEFT edge of the panel. Never draw the knob on the left side of the
  panel. Knob height: adult waist height.
- Never replace the knob with a latch, a bolt, a sliding lock, a lever
  handle, or an occupied/vacant indicator. Always the same round knob.
- GAP UNDER THE DOOR: the door does NOT reach the floor. There is an open
  gap about one hand's width tall between the bottom edge of the door and
  the floor, running the full width of the door. This gap is present in
  every scene that shows this door.
- Wall: door frame visible, wall continuing to the right behind the
  characters, a tiled band along the upper wall, and the join between
  wall and floor.
- NO urinals, no gendered fixtures, no toilet, no toilet bowl, no sink.

=== THE GAP IN THIS SCENE - DRAW IT, IT IS EASY TO MISS ===
The door must NOT touch the floor. Its bottom edge stops clearly ABOVE
the floor, leaving an open horizontal gap about one hand's width tall,
running the full width of the door. Through that gap the floor inside is
visible as a plain lighter strip.
The gap is EMPTY: no feet, no shoes, no stool - nobody is in there yet.
An earlier attempt drew the door reaching all the way down to the floor.
That is wrong. The gap must be plainly visible.

ACTION: Toton stretches up on tiptoe and knocks on the lower part of the
closed door with one small rounded hand. Two small motion marks near the
knocking hand. Mio stands just behind Toton on the right, watching,
hands at their sides.
Mood: reassuring, slightly playful. Knocking is the rule you follow
before you go in.

=== NO TEXT, NO STRAY SHAPES ===
Render no writing of any kind - no kana, kanji, letters or numbers.
The top third is plain empty background, left blank on purpose.
No floating or disembodied head, hair, hand or limb. No unexplained
blob, dome or hanging object. Every shape must be part of the room:
door, frame, wall, tiled band, floor, Toton, Mio.


=== THE DOOR IS SHUT. THIS KEEPS GOING WRONG. ===
The door is CLOSED, flat against its frame, its face turned toward the
viewer. You cannot see into the room behind it at all - no interior
wall, no floor inside, no fixtures, nothing.
Do NOT draw the door standing open. Do NOT draw it ajar or swung inward.
Do NOT draw a saloon-style half door or a short partition you can see
over. It is a full, solid, closed door.
Recent attempts drew this door hanging open, which destroys the point of
the scene.


=== WHAT THIS PICTURE MUST SHOW - READ THIS LAST ===
One closed door. Toton knocking on it. Mio waiting behind Toton.
That is the entire picture.

The last attempt produced something completely different: a door hanging
wide open with a toilet and a washbasin visible inside, and the child
holding a tissue and smiling. All of that is wrong.

Checklist for this image:
  - The door is SHUT. Flat. Facing the viewer. Nothing visible behind it.
  - NO toilet, NO toilet bowl, NO washbasin, NO sink, NO mirror,
    NO toilet paper anywhere in the picture.
  - Toton is knocking: one rounded hand raised to the lower part of the
    door, two small motion marks beside that hand.
  - Toton's mouth is a single flat horizontal line. Not smiling.
  - Mio stands behind Toton, on the right, arms down at the sides,
    holding nothing at all.
  - Mio's expression is uncertain, not happy.

=== OUTPUT SPEC ===
- Square 1:1 composition.
- Generate at the largest square size the model offers.
- Target for print: 2625 x 2625 px at 300 dpi
  (8.5 inch trim + 0.125 inch bleed on each side).
  If the model outputs smaller, upscale afterwards to 2625 px.
- Keep every important element at least 150 px inside all four edges.
  Nothing meaningful within 0.125 inch of the outer edge: that strip
  gets trimmed off in printing.
- TEXT SPACE: top third. That area must stay low-detail and close to a single
  even color, so Japanese text can be placed on it later. Do not put a
  face or any busy detail there.

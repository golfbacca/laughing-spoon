=== ABSOLUTE RULES ===
- Do NOT imitate, reference, or resemble any existing published picture
  book, existing character, franchise, or any named illustrator's style.
  This artwork must be fully original.
- Do NOT include brand names, trademarks, or recognizable products.
- Except where a scene explicitly asks for title text, render NO text,
  letters, numbers, logos, or signage anywhere in the image.

=== STYLE LOCK ===
- Soft, warm, bright children's picture-book illustration.
- Rounded shapes, soft edges, no sharp corners anywhere.
- Watercolor-and-colored-pencil feel: gentle visible texture over clean
  flat areas of color.
- High-key lighting. No harsh shadows, no heavy contrast, no darkness
  except where a scene says "slightly dim".
- Palette, fixed for the whole book: cream, pale sky blue, soft yellow,
  warm beige, muted green, soft coral. No neon. No black fills.
- Backgrounds simple and uncluttered. Suggest the place, do not fill it
  with detail.
- NOT anime. NOT manga. NOT 3D render. NOT photorealistic.
  NOT flat-vector-corporate. NOT glossy AI-look.

=== CHARACTER LOCK: "Toton" ===
Toton is an imaginary small creature. NOT based on any real animal
species and NOT based on any existing character.
- Body: rounded, like a slightly flattened egg. Almost no neck.
- Limbs: four short stubby limbs.
- Size: fits in a small child's palm (about one palm wide).
- Body color: pale sky blue. The belly area only is white.
- Cheeks: one small orange circle on each cheek. Present in EVERY image,
  in every mood. It is a marking, not a blush effect.
- Eyes: solid black dots only. No whites of the eyes, no eyelashes,
  no highlights, no eyebrows.
- Ears: two small round ears, set high on the head and wide apart.
- Mouth: one short horizontal line. When open, only a wide flat oval.
  Never show teeth or tongue.
- Tail: NONE, in any image.
- Hands and feet: rounded tips. Do not draw fingers or toes.
- Accessory: a small yellow backpack worn on its back. Never a bib,
  never an apron, never clothing.
- Inside the backpack: one white towel. It is visible ONLY in the
  handwashing scene.
- Expression rules, use ONLY these two:
    happy   -> ears twitch: one small motion mark beside each ear
    anxious -> both hands gripping the backpack straps
  At all other times Toton's face is calm and neutral.

=== CHARACTER LOCK: "Mio" (the child) ===
- A toddler about 3 years old. Gender-neutral: must not read as clearly
  a boy or clearly a girl.
- Short soft dark-brown hair, simple rounded silhouette.
- Round face, simple black dot eyes in the same style as Toton's,
  small simple mouth.
- Clothing, identical in every image: plain moss-green short-sleeve
  shirt, beige shorts, red sneakers, small cream-colored backpack.
- Mio is fully clothed in every image.

=== CHARACTER LOCK: "the grown-up" ===
- One adult companion. Never named, never shown as specifically a mother
  or a father. Keep the role neutral.
- Shoulder-length hair tied back, plain navy long-sleeve top,
  grey trousers.
- Usually partial: hands only, or from the shoulders down, or crouching
  with a calm gentle face. Never the focus of the image.

=== THIS IMAGE ===
Toton's head and shoulders poking out of the top of Mio's cream
backpack, which Mio is wearing. Both face the viewer. Mio smiling
calmly, Toton neutral. A park gate softly suggested behind them, kept
very simple. Warm, inviting, safe.

Composition: characters in the LOWER two-thirds. Leave the TOP third as
a calm, near-single-color area for the title.

TITLE TEXT — this is the one image that carries text:
Render this Japanese text across the top third, large and centered,
in a soft rounded hand-lettered style, dark warm brown, no outline,
no shadow, no effects:

    トトンと おでかけトイレ

Nothing else in writing. No subtitle, no author name, no other text.
The subtitle will be added by hand afterwards.

IF THE JAPANESE GLYPHS COME OUT WRONG (this is common): regenerate this
same image with the title line removed and no text at all, then place
the title by hand afterwards. Do not accept an image with malformed
Japanese characters — readers notice immediately.

=== OUTPUT SPEC ===
- Portrait, aspect ratio 1 : 1.6 (width : height). This is the Kindle
  cover ratio, not square. Do not output a square image.
- Generate at the largest size the model offers, then upscale so the
  final file is at least 1600 x 2560 px. Larger is fine if the ratio
  holds.
- Keep every important element well inside the frame; leave breathing
  room at all four edges.
- The paperback cover is assembled later as one wrapped file
  (back + spine + front). This image is the FRONT panel only.
